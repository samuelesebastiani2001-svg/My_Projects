# Course-Projects

Two empirical econometrics projects completed during my master's degree in Applied Economics.

## 1. [Time Series Econometrics – Group project](group8.pdf)

*Group project (5 members)*

An analysis of the long-run relationship between public consumption prices and two cost drivers, using quarterly data: the value-added deflator adjusted for indirect taxes, and hourly labour costs. The project covers:

- **Univariate analysis**: time-series plots, correlograms and ADF unit-root tests
- **Multivariate analysis**: VAR models on HP-filtered gaps and first differences, with impulse responses and diagnostic tests
- **Single-equation analysis**: an ARDL(4,4,4) model reparametrized as an error correction model (ECM), and the Engle-Granger cointegration test
- **Cointegrated VAR**: the Johansen cointegration test and a VECM, comparing its results with the single-equation findings

## 2. [Econometrics of Financial Markets – Empirical exam](Sebastiani_Samuele.pdf)

*Individual project, October 2025 · built in Gretl*

Three exercises on volatility and shock propagation in financial and macroeconomic data:

- **Time-varying volatility of an asset**: GARCH(1,1) and GARCH(2,2) compared with a likelihood-ratio test, the half-life of shocks, a Student-t GARCH for fat tails, and an asymmetric power GARCH (APARCH) to test for the leverage effect
- **Dynamic correlations between three assets**: DCC models with scalar and Cholesky specifications for the conditional correlation matrix
- **Propagation of shocks**: a VAR on oil prices, inflation and GDP growth, with structural impulse responses identified through Cholesky ordering
